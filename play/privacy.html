Web game privacy

Updated 30 October 2026. This page covers the RB Games browser version of PERIGEE PANIC.

Playing without an account

The free game needs no account. Preferences and game progress may be stored in your browser. If you submit a leaderboard score, the game sends your chosen name and score to GDevelop's leaderboard service. The website and game hosting providers receive the network information needed to deliver the game, such as your IP address.

Offline storage

Choosing Save for offline play downloads game files into your browser's storage. The browser can remove these files when storage is low or when you clear website data. Clearing website data can also remove local saves and sign you out.

Optional account and purchases

When enabled, optional accounts use Supabase to verify your email address and restore purchases. RB Games stores an account identifier and records of the cosmetic content you own. Stripe processes browser payments; this website does not receive or store your full card number. Google verifies linked Google Play purchases. Payment references and purchase tokens are handled by the backend to check ownership and refunds; purchase tokens are not exposed in your account page or included in offline caches.

Signing out removes this device's saved account session and offline purchase licence. A verified offline cosmetic licence, when issued, lasts up to 72 hours; restoring or buying requires an internet connection. An account is optional and does not improve gameplay.

Request deletion or support

Email dev.outpost503@passinbox.com to request account/data deletion or help with a purchase. Payment records that must be retained for legal or accounting reasons may be kept for the required period. Deleting an account does not automatically refund a purchase.

See also the game privacy policy and support page.
