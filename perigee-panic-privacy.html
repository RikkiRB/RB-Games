PERIGEE PANIC Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

PERIGEE PANIC is developed and published by RB Games.

RB Games respects your privacy and aims to collect only the minimum information required for the game to function.

Information we collect

PERIGEE PANIC does not require users to create an account or sign in.

If you choose to submit a score to the in-game leaderboard, the game sends:

  • the player name or nickname you entered
  • your game score

This information is used only to provide leaderboard functionality.

How the information is used

Leaderboard data is used to:

  • display player names and scores
  • rank submitted scores
  • provide the in-game leaderboard

RB Games does not use this information for advertising, profiling, targeted marketing, or selling user data.

Third-party services

PERIGEE PANIC uses GDevelop leaderboard services to process and store leaderboard submissions.

Leaderboard data is transmitted over an encrypted connection.

You can review GDevelop's privacy information on the GDevelop website.

PERIGEE PANIC uses GDevelop’s online leaderboard service. When a player submits a score, the player nickname and score are transmitted to GDevelop and displayed on the leaderboard. GDevelop may offer an optional “Claim your score” feature that allows a score to be associated with a GDevelop player account. Claiming a score is not required to play the game or appear on the leaderboard. GDevelop processes this information according to its own privacy policy.

Payments

Accounts

Children

PERIGEE PANIC is an arcade game intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children.

Data choices

Submitting a leaderboard score is optional. You may play the game without submitting your name or score.

Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard submissions may be retained by the leaderboard service in order to provide leaderboard functionality.

RB Games does not currently provide an automated in-game method for deleting leaderboard submissions.
