PERIGEE PANIC Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

PERIGEE PANIC is developed and published by RB Games. This policy describes the game and its optional account and purchase features. Features vary by version and platform. The free game does not require an RB Games account.

Information used by the game

  • Local game data: settings, progress, gold, cosmic diamonds, earned items and equipped cosmetics may be saved on your device or in browser storage. These earned items are not currently synchronised through the RB Games account. Clearing app or browser data may erase the local save.
  • Leaderboard: if you submit a score, your chosen player name and score are sent to GDevelop's leaderboard service and may be displayed publicly. You can play without submitting a score.
  • Optional account: when you sign in, we use your email address and an account identifier to authenticate you and associate verified purchases with your account. Sign-in uses a code sent to your email. An account session is stored on the device or in the browser until you sign out, it expires, or local data is cleared.
  • Purchases: where available, optional paid content can be purchased through the relevant store or browser checkout. To link and restore purchases, our service processes the product, store, purchase reference, verification token or receipt, purchase status and account identifier. These records help us verify ownership and handle refunds or fraud. The stores and payment providers process payment details; RB Games does not receive your full card number or store password.
  • Support and technical information: if you contact us, we receive the information you provide. Hosting and service providers may process network and diagnostic information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure their services.

How we use and share information

We use this information to run the game, provide the leaderboard, sign you in, verify and restore purchases, prevent fraud, respond to support requests and maintain security. We use service providers for these purposes, including GDevelop for leaderboards, Supabase for account and backend services, email delivery providers for sign-in codes, Google Play and Apple for store purchases, and Stripe for browser payments where available. These providers process information needed for their services and may have their own privacy policies.

The game contains no advertising. The RB Games website may offer optional analytics separately; see website privacy for details. The web game privacy page also describes browser-specific features.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and purchase-link records while needed to provide account and restoration services, unless you request deletion. Support correspondence and technical logs may be kept as needed to provide support, protect the service or meet legal obligations. Payment providers may retain their own transaction records under their policies and applicable obligations.

In versions that provide RB Games accounts, open the account screen, choose Account settings and follow the account deletion instructions. To request deletion without the app, email dev.outpost503@passinbox.com with the subject “PERIGEE PANIC account deletion”. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Deleting the account removes the RB Games account and its linked purchase records; it does not automatically refund purchases or delete local game progress. Restoring a linked web purchase after account deletion may require support.

Leaderboard records are managed through GDevelop's service. Contact us if you need help with a leaderboard or privacy request. Local data can be removed through your device or browser settings, but doing so may also remove saved progress.

Your choices and contact

Account use and leaderboard submissions are optional. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact dev.outpost503@passinbox.com with questions or requests. You may also contact your relevant data protection authority.

PERIGEE PANIC is a general-audience arcade game and is not specifically directed at children. We may update this policy when the game or its services change.
