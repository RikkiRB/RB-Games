Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

PERIGEE PANIC is designed as a standalone arcade game. RB Games does not intentionally collect, sell, rent, or trade personal information through the game.

Data Collection

PERIGEE PANIC does not require an account, login, or profile to play.

The game itself does not intentionally request or store personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

Platform and Technical Data

Apple, Google, the operating system, app stores, hosting providers, or other platform services may independently process limited technical information such as crash reports, diagnostics, device information, download information, or network data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

If future versions of PERIGEE PANIC add analytics, advertising, online services, account features, or other third-party integrations that collect user data, this privacy policy will be updated before or alongside those changes.

Children

PERIGEE PANIC does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because RB Games does not intentionally collect personal information through the game, there is normally no personal game-account data held by RB Games to retain or delete. If you contact support directly, correspondence may be retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or maintain support records.
