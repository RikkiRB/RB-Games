I'm the developer behind RB Games. Based in Cologne, originally from London. I build games that follow the idea — from orbital arcade action to interactive stories and puzzles.

That also means building the tools behind the games. StoryDev is my visual novel framework, with After The Last Train as a playable example of what it can do.
