Website privacy

Updated 2 November 2026. This page covers optional analytics on the RB Games homepage and PERIGEE PANIC browser launch page.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, RB Games uses HeyCatch to understand visits and how people find the website and game. HeyCatch processes page and session events, referral and campaign information, browser/device details, IP addresses and pseudonymous browser identifiers. Analytics is off until you choose Allow analytics. Rejecting it does not limit the game.

We disable automatic click and form capture and mask captured text and element attributes. We do not identify your game account to HeyCatch or send your email, sign-in codes, payment details or leaderboard entries. Our integration removes query strings and fragments from event URLs and reduces referrer URLs to their origin. HeyCatch also redacts authentication tokens. Campaign attribution is handled separately by the SDK. The tracker is not installed inside the game iframe.

Your choice and browser storage

The site stores your analytics choice in this browser. If you allow analytics, HeyCatch can store its own identifiers locally. You can withdraw permission using Analytics settings in the website footer, game footer, or installed game's App options. Withdrawal stops further collection; clearing this site's browser data also removes local identifiers and may remove game saves and sign you out. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals keep analytics off.

Providers and requests

HeyCatch processes analytics on behalf of RB Games using its service providers, including processing in the United States. See HeyCatch's privacy policy, data processing addendum and sub-processors for provider information and retention. To ask about your analytics data or request deletion, email dev.outpost503@passinbox.com.

Game and account data

The hosting service receives network information needed to deliver the website. The web game privacy page covers saves, offline storage, optional sign-in, leaderboards and purchases. The existing game-specific policies continue to cover their respective games.
